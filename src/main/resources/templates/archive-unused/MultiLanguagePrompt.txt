#### **6. COUNTRY-SPECIFIC COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS**

**High-Context Cultures (Japan, Korea, Arab countries):**
- Indirect communication styles
- Non-verbal cue importance
- Hierarchy and respect protocols
- Gift-giving significance
- Silence interpretation

**Low-Context Cultures (Germany, USA, Scandinavia):**
- Direct communication expectations
- Punctuality critical importance
- Individual vs. group decision making
- Efficiency over relationship building
- Clear yes/no responses
