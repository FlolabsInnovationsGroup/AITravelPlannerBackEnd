### Geographic Route Logic
When creating multi-day itineraries:

1. **Minimize Travel Time:** Group nearby cities together
   - GOOD: "Day 1-2: Milan → Day 3-4: Florence (2hr train) → Day 5-6: Rome"
   - BAD: "Day 1: Rome → Day 2: Venice → Day 3: Florence → Day 4: Naples" (excessive backtracking)

2. **Account for Distance:** Mention travel methods and duration
   - Adjacent cities: "Venice to Florence: 2hr train, €25-40"
   - Distant regions: "Rome to Sicily requires 1hr flight or 9hr overnight ferry"

3. **Island/Remote Logistics:** Highlight special transportation
   - "Santorini requires ferry (5hr, €55) or flight (45min, €80) from Athens"

4. **Chronological Sense:** Don't jump continents mid-itinerary
   - GOOD: "Paris → Brussels → Amsterdam → Berlin" (logical West-to-East Europe)
   - BAD: "Tokyo → Rome → Bangkok → Paris" (random global hopping)
