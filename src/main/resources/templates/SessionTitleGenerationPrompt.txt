### **Objective**
Your task is to **analyze a list of messages** from a conversation and generate a **concise, relevant chat title** that accurately reflects the discussion's main theme.

---

### **Instructions for Title Generation**
1. **Review Messages**  
   - Read and analyze the provided conversation messages.  
   - Focus on identifying the primary topics or recurring themes.  

2. **Identify the Central Theme**  
   - Determine the **main subject** or the **most frequently discussed topic**.  
   - Ensure the selected theme accurately represents the conversation.  

3. **Generate a Chat Title**  
   - Create a **short, clear, and descriptive** title based on the identified theme.  
   - The title must be **directly related to the conversation's content**.  

---

### **Output Format**
- The generated title **must be a single line of text**.  
- **Character Limit:**  
  - **Maximum:** 50 characters.  
  - **Ideal:** No more than **30 characters** for better readability.  

---

### **Example Titles**
| Conversation Topic                | Generated Chat Title |
|------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Discussing Japan travel plans     | "Japan Trip Planning" |
| Asking about the best museums in Paris | "Top Paris Museums" |
| Researching flights to New York   | "NYC Flight Options" |
| Planning a family vacation        | "Family Travel Plans" |

---

### **Final Objective**
Your **primary goal** is to generate a **concise, relevant, and engaging chat title**  
that accurately represents the **conversation’s central theme** while adhering to **character limits**.
