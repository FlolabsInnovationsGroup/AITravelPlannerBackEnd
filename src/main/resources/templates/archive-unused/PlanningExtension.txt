## PLANNING BEST PRACTICES

### 1. GEOGRAPHIC OPTIMIZATION
- Group nearby venues together
- Minimize backtracking
- Account for transit time between locations
- Route: Start → nearby → nearby → end (not random jumping)

**Example:**
✅ GOOD: "Eiffel Tower (7th) → Seine walk → Louvre (1st) → Le Marais (3rd)" (West to East flow)
❌ BAD: "Eiffel Tower (7th) → Montmartre (18th) → Louvre (1st) → Versailles (outside)" (chaotic)

### 2. TIMING INTELLIGENCE
- **Morning (9am-12pm)**: Major attractions before crowds
- **Lunch (12pm-2pm)**: Prix-fixe menu savings
- **Afternoon (2pm-6pm)**: Museums, shopping, exploration
- **Evening (7pm-10pm)**: Dining, entertainment, nightlife

Include:
- Best time to arrive at each venue
- How long to spend
- Buffer time for unexpected delays
- Rest/break periods (don't over-schedule!)

### 3. DAY STRUCTURE VARIETY
- Don't repeat same pattern every day
- Mix: active days + relaxing days
- Cultural days + nature days
- Structured mornings + flexible afternoons
- **Day 1**: Heavy tourism (jet lag energy boost)
- **Mid-trip**: Slower pace options
- **Final day**: Leave time for airport/checkout

### 4. DETAILED VENUE INFORMATION

For EVERY venue include:
- ✅ Full name + complete address
- ✅ Exact price (not "moderate" or "expensive")
- ✅ Current hours (today + full week if different)
- ✅ Time allocation recommendation
- ✅ Booking requirements (advance, walk-in, etc.)
- ✅ ONE specific insider tip
- ✅ Rating + review count

### 5. TRANSPORTATION DETAILS
- Exact transit lines/routes
- Walking time + distance
- Cost per segment
- Alternative options
- Peak vs off-peak timing
