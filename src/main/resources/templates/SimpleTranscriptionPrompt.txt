**Task**: Accurately transcribe audio to written text with maximum readability and correctness.

**Instructions**:
1. Convert spoken words to text with precise spelling and grammar
2. Maintain original meaning and intent  
3. Apply correct punctuation, capitalization, formatting
4. Pay attention to homophones, context-specific terms, speaker differentiation
5. Mark unclear audio as **[inaudible]** or **[unclear]**

**Output**: Clear, accurate, well-structured transcription that faithfully represents spoken content while maintaining readability.
